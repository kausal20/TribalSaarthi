Why it fits

  • Text is a few hundred bytes, so it works on weak networks
  • No smartphone needed for the SMS version
  • Reuses what is already built: matcher, checklist, Hindi and Marathi
  • Tribal languages next, using Bhashini or a similar language service

What it needs first NOT BUILT

  • WhatsApp Business account and approved message templates
  • An SMS gateway, with sender and template registration as Indian rules require
  • The same safety rule: never ask for Aadhaar, OTP or passwords in chat
  • A pilot with a school or NGO before any claim about reach
